About Me

I work on wireless positioning for digital car key systems, with a focus on Bluetooth Channel Sounding (CS) ranging. My work centers on making distance estimation accurate and robust in real vehicle environments — especially under non-line-of-sight (NLOS) and multipath conditions.

My background spans signal processing, state estimation (e.g. Kalman filtering), and embedded implementation under automotive-grade constraints, within standards ecosystems such as CCC Digital Key 3.0/4.0 and ICCE. I am increasingly interested in applying AI / machine learning to wireless sensing problems.
